## Batch 4: Pages 16-20

### Page 16 (Slide 19): Protocol Steps and Timing
- **Overview:** Flexible 2-day workflow for processing 2 tissue slides with a single CytAssist instrument.
- **Workflow Details:**
    - **Day 0 (Variable):**
        - Freeze Fresh Tissue $\rightarrow$ Stop: -80°C long-term*
        - Embed Tissue in OCT $\rightarrow$ Stop: -80°C long-term*
        - Quality Assessment $\rightarrow$ Stop: -80°C for $\le$ 4 weeks*
        - Sectioning & Placement
    - **Day 1 (~6.5 h):**
        - Reagent Preparation (~30 m)
        - Slide Prep & Fixation (~20 m)
        - H&E Staining, Coverslip Mounting (~15 m) $\rightarrow$ **Stop: 4°C $\le$ 24 h**
        - Imaging (<1 h) $\rightarrow$ **Stop: 4°C $\le$ 72 h**
        - HD Slide & CytAssist Preparation (~1 h)
        - Coverslip Removal & Destaining (~40 m)
        - Poly(A) RNA Release & Capture (~1 h)
        - Reverse Transcription & Denaturation (~2 h)
        - Second Strand Synthesis (~1 h) $\rightarrow$ **Stop: 4°C $\le$ 24 h**
    - **Day 2 (~6 h):**
        - Second Strand Elution (10 m)
        - cDNA Amplification (~20 m) $\rightarrow$ **Stop: 4°C for $\le$ 24 h**
        - SPRI Cleanup (~20 m) $\rightarrow$ **Stop: 4°C for $\le$ 72 h or -20°C for $\le$ 4 weeks**
        - cDNA QC (~1 h)
        - Fragmentation, End Repair, A-tailing & Cleanup (~2 h)
        - Sample Index PCR (~30 m) $\rightarrow$ **Stop: 4°C for $\le$ 72 h**
        - SPRI Cleanup (~30 m) $\rightarrow$ **Stop: 4°C for $\le$ 72 h or -20°C long term**
        - Library QC (~1 h)
- **Note:** *Storage should be in an airtight container.

### Page 21 (Slide 24): Compatible Tissue Features
- **Requirements:**
    - Most Fresh frozen; OCT embedded (FF) tissue blocks.
    - Tissue section thickness: **$10\text{ }\mu\text{m}$**.
    - RNA Quality: **RIN $\ge$ 7** recommended.
- **Tested Tissues:** Long list of compatible tissues for Human, Mouse, and other species.
- **Challenging Tissues:** Human lung (Healthy) noted as challenging.

### Page 22 (Slide 25): Tissue Slide Preparation
- **Freezing & Embedding Methods:**
    1. **Simultaneous Freezing & Embedding:** Fresh tissue $\rightarrow$ OCT $\rightarrow$ Isopentane over Dry Ice.
    2. **Separate Freezing & Embedding:** Fresh tissue $\rightarrow$ Isopentane over Dry Ice $\rightarrow$ OCT $\rightarrow$ Isopentane over Dry Ice.
- **Warning:** **DO NOT freeze tissues directly in liquid nitrogen.**
- **Storage:** -80°C for long term. Cryosections can be stored at -80°C for up to 4 weeks.

### Page 23 (Slide 28): Tissue Slide Shipping
- **Instructions:**
    - Place slides in pre-chilled slide mailer sitting in dry ice.
    - Ensure side(s) with tissue face inward and sufficient space between slides.
    - Alternative: $50\text{ ml}$ centrifuge tube (one slide per tube).
- **Warning:** Temperature fluctuations may result in RNA degradation.

### Page 24 (Slide 29): RNA Quality Assessment (RIN)
- **Procedure:**
    - Collect $20-30\text{ mg}$ of tissue sections.
    - Perform RNA extraction.
    - Measure concentration (Nanodrop/Qubit).
    - Evaluate RIN.
- **Requirement:** Protocol optimized for **RIN $\ge$ 7**.

### Page 25 (Slide 30): Tissue Morphology Assessment
- **Goal:** Ensure tissue section is free from processing and sectioning artifacts.
- **Common Artifacts:** Improper snap freezing, squeeze/crush, ice crystals, hemorrhaging, cracks, oxidized blood, tissue necrosis, bubbles, and folds.
- **Impact:** Affected regions are unlikely to generate good data.

### Page 33 (Slide 38): Step 1: Visium HD Slide & Instrument Preparation
- **Visium HD Slide Preparation Steps:**
    1. **Remove mylar bag** containing HD slide from -80°C.
    2. **Thaw slide mailer** sitting upright with cap on at RT (30 m - 3 h).
    3. **Remove cap** from mailer.
    4. **Wash slide in mailer**, ensuring capture areas are completely submerged.
    5. **Hold slide securely** while discarding buffer.
    6. **Wick remaining liquid** using a lint-free laboratory wipe.
    7. **Inspect to ensure slide is free of debris**.
       - Immerse slide quickly in and out of buffer in mailer to remove.
    8. **Record the Slide Serial Number**.
    9. **Assemble in Visium Cassette S3**.
    10. **Wash cassette wells with buffer**, removing ALL liquid.
    11. **Add Pre-equilibration Mix and seal cassette**.
        - DO NOT exceed 1 hour before initiating CytAssist run.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - **Thaw at room temperature:** Slide mailer with cap on.
    - **Add buffer to both sides of slide:** Pipette adding liquid to mailer.
    - **Incubate with cap off:** Mailer open. Note: "Washes: 1x 1 min, 3x 5 min".
    - **Carefully dump holding slide in place:** Hand holding mailer and dumping liquid.
    - **Wick remaining liquid from slide:** Slide on a lint-free wipe.
    - **Add/remove into bottom right corner:** Pipette adding liquid to cassette.

### Page 34 (Slide 39): Step 2: Coverslip Removal & Destaining (Removal)
- **Tissue Slide Coverslip Removal Steps:**
    1. **Ensure beakers have been cleaned** per recommendations.
    2. **Remove coverslip** by incubating slide in water while holding sideways until slowly separates away.
    3. **Immerse slide 30x** to remove residual mounting media.
- **Warnings:**
    - DO NOT move slide up and down or shake forcibly or manually move coverslip.
    - Ensure mounting media is fully removed. Residual media will likely affect downstream steps.
- **Tip:** Utilize the Visium Alignment Aid Accessory prior to removing the coverslip.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - **Immerse:** Slide in beaker with water.
    - **Hold:** Holding slide sideways (horizontally) in water.
    - **Coverslip Detaches:** Coverslip falling off slide in water.

### Page 35 (Slide 40): Step 2: Coverslip Removal & Destaining (Destaining)
- **Tissue Slide Destaining Steps:**
    1. **Retrieve tissue slides** from 4°C.
    2. **Remove Hematoxylin** from tissue.
    3. **Start preparation of Permeabilization Mix**.
    4. **Immerse & incubate slides in PBS**.
    5. **Flick the tissue slides** until all buffer has been dislodged from surface of tissue.
    6. **Place slides on lint-free wipe**.
- **Warnings:**
    - Ensure tissue sections are completely submerged.
    - DO NOT make complete Permeabilization Mix.
    - DO NOT exceed 5 min in PBS buffer after destaining.
- **Tip:** Utilize the Visium Alignment Aid Accessory prior to removing the coverslip.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - **Slide Placement in Slide Mailer:** "Face tissue sections toward each other." "Ensure sufficient space in between slides."
    - **Flick the Slide:** "Lead with the elbow and allow the wrist to rotate."

## Batch 8: Pages 36-40

### Page 36 (Slide 41): Step 3: Poly(A) RNA Capture, RT, & Denaturation (Capture)
- **CytAssist-Enabled Poly(A) RNA Release & Capture Details:**
    - Visium CytAssist instrument running firmware **v2.0.0 or higher**:
      - During the run, the tissue is permeabilized releasing poly(A) RNA.
      - The released poly(A) RNA is captured by the oligo poly(dT)VN sequence on the Visium HD slide directly below.
      - It is normal for tissue to remain on the Tissue Slide after this step.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - **CytAssist-Enabled Poly(A) RNA Capture:** Instrument with slide, zoomed-in view of cells releasing RNA (blue lines) to slide surface with oligos (green).
    - **Poly(A) RNA Capture Molecule Structure:** 3' Poly(dT)VN (Blue), Spatial Barcode (Green), UMI (Orange), Read 1 (Black), connected to Slide (5').
    - **Capture Mechanism:** Tissue RNA 3' AAAAAAAAAA... binding to Slide Oligo TTTTTTTTTT 3'.

### Page 37 (Slide 42): Step 3: Poly(A) RNA Capture, RT, & Denaturation (RT)
- **Reverse Transcription & Denaturation Details:**
    - RT Master Mix is added to the Visium HD Slide. This produces spatially barcoded, full-length cDNA from poly(A) RNA captured on the slide.
    - **Two rounds of Reverse Transcription are required.**
    - Captured poly(A) RNA is denatured and washed away, leaving first strand cDNA attached to HD slide via barcoded oligos.
- **Warnings:**
    - Ensure KOH is prepared fresh at 0.08M.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - **Reverse Transcription:** RNA 3' AAAAA extended with cDNA ending in "ccc".
    - **Template Switch Oligo Priming:** TSO (Green, "rGrGrG") binds to "ccc".
    - **Extension:** cDNA extended to copy TSO.
    - **Denaturation:** RNA removed, cDNA remains attached.

### Page 38 (Slide 43): Step 4: Second Strand Synthesis & Elution
- **Details:**
    - Second strand mix is added to first strand cDNA attached to the HD slide to generate double stranded cDNA.
    - **STOP:** 4°C for ≤ 24 hours.
    - Newly synthesized second strand cDNA is eluted off the slide, collected in an 8-well strip tube and neutralized.
- **Warnings:**
    - Ensure KOH is prepared fresh at 0.08M.
    - Ensure 1M Tris-HCl pH 7.0 is used for neutralization.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - **Second Strand Synthesis Priming:** Second Strand Primer (Green) binds near TSO.
    - **Second Strand Synthesis:** Primer extended to create second strand.
    - **Second Strand Elution:** Strand eluted off.
    - **Final Product Structure:** Read 1, UMI, Spatial Barcode, Poly(dT)VN, Insert, TSO.

### Page 39 (Slide 44): Step 5: cDNA Amplification & SPRIselect
- **Details:**
    - Spatially barcoded, single stranded cDNA is amplified with a fixed number of cycles to generate additional product.
    - **STOP:** 4°C for ≤ 24 hours.
    - Amplified cDNA is then cleaned up with single sided SPRIselect.
    - **STOP:** 4°C for ≤ 72 hours, -20°C for ≤ 4 weeks.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - **cDNA Amplification:** Shows "pRead 1" and "TSO" primers. Arrow to "cDNA Amplification". Shows double stranded product.

### Page 40 (Slide 45): Step 5: cDNA Amplification & SPRIselect (QC)
- **cDNA QC & Quantification Details:**
    - cDNA is diluted **1:5**, may not be optimal for all samples.
      - Ensure concentration is within the specification range of electrophoresis assay kit.
    - cDNA concentration and average size is determined using a Bioanalyzer or similar automated electrophoresis instrument.
      - cDNA average size expected **~500-1000bp**.
      - cDNA yield calculated to determine number of cycles to use during Sample Index PCR step.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - **Bioanalyzer (1:5 dilution):** Two traces showing size distribution. Broad peaks ranging from ~300 to ~2000 bp.

## Batch 9: Pages 41-45

### Page 41 (Slide 46): Step 6: Library Construction (Fragmentation, End Repair, & A-tailing)
- **Details:**
    - Enzymatic Fragmentation followed by double sided SPRI are performed to optimize cDNA amplicon size.
    - **25% of cDNA is used as input**.
    - End Repair & A-tailing prepare the 3' end.
    - Adaptor ligation adds a partial Read 2 sequence and is followed by a single sided SPRI.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - **Input:** Read 1, UMI, Spatial Barcode, Poly(dT)VN, Insert, TSO.
    - **Enzymatic Fragmentation:** TSO end cut off.
    - **End Repair, A-tailing, Ligation:** Read 2 ligated to insert end.
    - **Cleanup & Priming:** Ready for PCR.

### Page 42 (Slide 47): Step 6: Library Construction (Sample Index PCR)
- **Details:**
    - Spatially barcoded cDNA are amplified to add Sequencing Adapters (P5, P7) and Sample indexes (i5, i7).
    - Requires Dual Index Plate TT Set A containing 1 unique i5 and i7 index per well.
    - PCR cycle number based on fragmentation reaction cDNA input mass (25% of cDNA yield).
    - **STOP:** 4°C for ≤ 72 hours.
    - PCR products then undergo doubled sided SPRIselect cleanup.
    - **STOP:** 4°C for ≤ 72 hours, -20°C for long term storage.
- **cDNA Input vs Total Cycles Table:**
    - 1-10 ng: 15 cycles
    - 10-50 ng: 14 cycles
    - 50-100 ng: 13 cycles
    - 100-250 ng: 12 cycles
    - 250-350 ng: 11 cycles
    - 350-600 ng: 10 cycles
    - 600-800 ng: 9 cycles
    - 800-1,100 ng: 8 cycles
    - 1,100-1,300 ng: 7 cycles
    - 1,300-1,500 ng: 6 cycles
    - >1,500 ng: 5 cycles
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - Primers: P5 - Sample Index (i5) and Sample Index (i7) - P7.
    - Final library construct: P5 - i5 - Read 1 - UMI - Spatial Barcode - Poly(dT)VN - Insert - Read 2 - i7 - P7.

### Page 43 (Slide 48): Step 6: Library Construction (Post-Library Construction QC)
- **Details:**
    - Library is diluted **1:5**, may not be optimal for all samples.
    - Quality and size is assessed using a Bioanalyzer or similar automated electrophoresis instrument.
      - Expect sharp peak **~450 bp** in size.
      - Additional peaks can indicate inadequate dilution and/or poor SPRIselect cleanup.
    - **KAPA qPCR library quantification** is performed to calculate sequenceable molecules containing P5 and P7 adaptors.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - Traces from Bioanalyzer, LabChip, and TapeStation all showing a sharp peak around 450 bp.

### Page 44 (Slide 49): Visium HD 3' Spatial Gene Expression (Sequencing Configuration)
- **Recommended Sequencing Configuration & Parameters Table:**
    - Paired-end, Dual Index Sequencing
    - **Read 1:** Purpose: UMI + Spatial Barcode, Cycles: 43
    - **i7:** Purpose: Sample Index, Cycles: 10
    - **i5:** Purpose: Sample Index, Cycles: 10
    - **Read 2:** Purpose: Insert, Cycles: 75
- **Note:** *Do not recommend pooling with other libraries.
- **Visuals/Diagrams:**
    - Library Construct diagram showing positions of P5, i5, Read 1, UMI, Spatial Barcode, Poly(dT)VN, Insert, Read 2, i7, P7.

### Page 45 (Slide 50): Visium HD 3' Spatial Gene Expression (Sequencing Depth Calculation)
- **Details:**
    - Estimate fraction of capture area covered by tissue by visual observation.
    - Minimum sequencing depth = (550 million read pairs * Fraction of capture area covered).
    - Alternatively, determine using manual workflow in Loupe Browser (v8.0 or higher).
- **Minimum Sequencing Depth Recommendation:**
    - **550 million read pairs per fully covered capture area**.
- **Examples:**
    - ~50% tissue coverage (Mouse Brain): 550M * 0.50 = 275M read pairs.
    - ~95% Tissue coverage (Human Colon Cancer): 550M * 0.95 = 523M read pairs.
